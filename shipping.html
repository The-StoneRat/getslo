Shipping Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Your prints are made to order in India and sent to the address you enter in the app.

1. Dispatch time

We print and dispatch your order within 3 business days of payment. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in India.

2. Shipping within India

  • We ship by India Post Speed Post.
  • Delivery usually takes 2 to 7 business days after dispatch, depending on your city.
  • The shipping cost depends on your location and pack size. It is shown at checkout before you pay.

3. Shipping outside India

  • We ship abroad by India Post Speed Post (international). International ordering is being added; if you can order to your country, the app will let you enter an address there.
  • Delivery usually takes 7 to 21 business days after dispatch. Customs checks can add time.
  • The shipping cost for your country is shown at checkout before you pay.
  • Customs duties, import taxes and any fees charged by your country are paid by you, the customer. They are not included in our price.

4. Tracking

Every order goes by Speed Post, which has a tracking number. Email us for yours and you can follow the parcel on the India Post website.

5. Your address

Please check your address before paying. If a parcel cannot be delivered because the address was wrong or incomplete, see our Refund and Cancellation Policy.

6. Late or lost parcels

Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees. If your order has not arrived within 30 days of dispatch, email us and we will sort it out as set out in our Refund and Cancellation Policy.
