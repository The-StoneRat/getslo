Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Every Slo order is printed from your own photos, just for you. That shapes what we can and cannot take back. Here is how it works.

1. Cancelling an order

  • Before printing starts: email us within 24 hours of paying and we will cancel the order and refund you in full.
  • After printing starts: the order can no longer be cancelled, because the prints are made to order.

2. Damaged, wrong or missing prints

If your prints arrive damaged, misprinted, or with photos missing, we will reprint them for free or refund you, whichever you prefer.

  • Email us within 7 days of delivery.
  • Include your order details and a photo of the problem.

3. Lost in the post

If your order has not arrived within 30 days of dispatch, tell us. We will check with the courier. If the parcel is lost, we will reprint and resend it for free or refund you in full.

4. What we cannot refund

  • Change of mind once printing has started.
  • How a photo looks when the print matches the photo you took, for example blur, framing or low light.
  • Parcels that cannot be delivered because the address given was wrong or incomplete. We can resend once you pay the shipping again.
  • Customs duties or import taxes charged outside India.

5. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. Once we approve a refund, it usually reaches you within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank.
